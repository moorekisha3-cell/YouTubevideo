## Intent

A YouTube Shorts cut of "The Retirement Tax Trap Most People Discover Too Late" for
the finance-education channel Retirement Wealth 101. Warm, trustworthy, calm,
educational tone — a plain-English financial educator, not a salesperson, not
flashy or scam-adjacent. Teaches that Required Minimum Distributions (RMDs)
starting at 73 can push retirees into a higher tax bracket, make more Social
Security taxable, and raise Medicare premiums — but it's avoidable with planning
(Roth conversions, tax diversification).

## Customizations

- Silent build: no narration audio and no BGM. No HeyGen sign-in is reachable
  from this session (network policy blocks api.heygen.com) and no offline
  TTS/music engines are installed. The on-screen kinetic typography carries
  the full message — each frame's text IS what a voiceover would have said,
  paced with fixed reading-time durations instead of audio-synced captions.
- End frame must include a small on-screen disclaimer: "Educational only. Not
  financial, tax, or legal advice."
- Design should read as trustworthy financial education (calm blues/greens,
  not neon or hype-driven).
