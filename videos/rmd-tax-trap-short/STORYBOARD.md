## Video direction

**Palette system** (from `frame.md`, Blue Professional): warm cream `bg` throughout as the ground; cobalt `primary` (#1e2bfa) is the only accent through Frames 1–2 (hook + rising stakes); the palette's `positive` green enters at Frame 3's "good news" turn and carries through Frame 4's calm close. Text on near-black `text` / muted `text-muted` per the pack's reading ladder. Never invent colors outside these tokens.

**Motion grammar + reveal model**: long-tail `power3` eases everywhere (smooth, never bouncy) except each blueprint's one signature spring-pop moment (the RMD payoff card, the icon chips, the wordmark assembly) — those alone may use a spring ease, per that blueprint's own definition. This is a **silent** piece (no VO, no SCRIPT.md) — every frame paces its reveals to an invented reading beat instead of spoken cues, following the same discipline: nothing dumps at t=0, each further piece of text/graphic reveals in its own window, spread across the frame's full duration. During any hold, at most a subtle jitter — no lazy breathing, no forced camera drift.

**Rhythm / held-frame allocation**: Frames 1–2 stay in continuous build (word swaps, icon count-ons) since they carry the problem and need momentum. Frame 3's final ~3.5s and Frame 4's final ~3s are the video's deliberate held breather beats — the "good news" card and the disclaimer both need to sit still long enough to read comfortably at an 8th-grade pace for a 50–75 audience. Do not add motion to fill those holds.

**Negative list**: no off-brand neon/purple-blue "AI gradient" clichés; no flashing or strobing; no fake urgency countdowns or red alarm color; no nav bars, browser chrome, or fake cursors; no slideshow failure (front-load-then-freeze — every frame must show its paced build, not the whole canvas at t=0); no screensaver failure (elements drifting independently with no shared composition). Frame 4's disclaimer line "Educational only. Not financial, tax, or legal advice." must render legibly and hold for at least 3s. All primary content across all four frames stays within the top ~83% of the canvas (caption-band keep-out), with a centered hero anchored at y ≈ 0.42 × height (≈806px at 1080×1920).
