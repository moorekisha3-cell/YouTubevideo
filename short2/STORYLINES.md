# Economics 1144 Short: three storylines

Vertical 1080×1920, 30 fps, 30–45 s. Music and sound effects, no voiceover by default.
Every number comes from the channel's own scripts.

## A. Your Number (recommended)

**Device:** a number lands. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds.
**Source:** `script_video3.md` (the 4% Rule and the 25× Rule).

One huge figure fills the phone screen and keeps rebuilding itself. Its digits roll,
split and multiply until the viewer has worked out their own retirement number.
It feels like a calculator answering a question they were afraid to ask.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 already shows **"$40,000 a year"** in giant type, with
  "How much do you need to retire?" above it. At 1 s a "× 25" slams in beside it.
- **Beats:** the question → × 25 rolls up to **$1,000,000** → flip it round: 4% of $1M =
  $40,000 a year → swap the input: $30,000 → $750,000, $60,000 → $1.5M →
  Social Security covers $20,000, so a $50,000 goal needs only $750,000 →
  claim: "Yearly spending × 25 = your number" → end card.
- **Look:** a deep green field with off-white numerals in a heavy condensed sans. A
  gold accent is used once, on the final number. The only "material" is the numbers
  themselves: rolling odometer digits.
- **Sound:** a low, steady pulse; a mechanical tick on each digit roll; a soft
  "lock" sound when a number lands.

## B. The $10,000 That Pays You

**Device:** a character does the work. **Form:** an object-to-object chain.
**Source:** `script_video7.md` (dividends and DRIP reinvesting).

A single $10,000 stack sits on a table. Each year it puts out a dividend coin, the coin
rolls back into the stack, and the stack grows. Every shot ends on the object that
opens the next one.

- **First 2 s:** a $10,000 bundle drops onto the table with a thud, and the caption
  reads "This $10,000 pays you every year."
- **Beats:** the stack → a $400 coin pops out (year 1) → the coin rolls back in, giving
  $10,400 → next year's coin is bigger, $416 → a 20-year time-lapse, coins pouring →
  "over $21,000 without adding a dollar" → the stack becomes a calendar: "$500/month
  takes about $150,000 at 4%" → end card.
- **Look:** a warm wood tabletop seen from above, with paper-cut money and coins in soft
  shadow. Type is a rounded sans in deep navy.
- **Sound:** a light marimba groove, coin clinks on each payout, and a rising whoosh
  under the time-lapse.

## C. Pay the Tax Now (the bold one)

**Device:** life before and after. **Form:** split screen to one.
**Source:** `script_video9.md` and `script_video4.md` (Roth IRA vs. Traditional IRA).

The screen splits top and bottom: two identical jars of savings growing over decades.
At retirement the top jar (Traditional) gets a tax bite taken out, while the bottom
jar (Roth) pays out whole. Then the split collapses into the Roth jar alone.

- **First 2 s:** two jars, one labelled "Traditional" and one "Roth", with the caption
  "Same savings. One gets taxed later."
- **Beats:** both jars fill side by side → age 59½ → the Traditional jar's bite lands →
  the Roth jar: "$0 tax on withdrawals" → the split collapses to the Roth jar →
  "$7,000 a year limit, $583 a month hits it" → end card.
  The jar's growth is illustrative and is labelled that way on screen.
- **Look:** a clean split with two tints (muted slate on top, bright teal below), glass
  jars with coins inside, and big bold sans titles.
- **Sound:** a tense, ticking build that resolves into a bright chord when the split
  collapses.
